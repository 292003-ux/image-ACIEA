  • GWP-WA
    Institutional partners

    Global Water Partnership West Africa (GWP-WA)

    Memorandum of Understanding · Signed on 3 July 2026

    On 3 July 2026, ACIEA and GWP West Africa signed a Memorandum of Understanding at the GWP-WA Secretariat in Ouagadougou. The partnership covers sustainable water resource management, climate resilience and digital innovation, centred on the deployment of the SAAGDO solution.

    Areas of collaboration

    • Sustainable water resource management
    • Climate resilience
    • Digital innovation
    • Deployment of SAAGDO (environmental monitoring, citizen mapping, pollution risk prediction)

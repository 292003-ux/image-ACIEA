Innovating for the climate.Acting for a sustainable future.

Based in Ouagadougou, ACIEA is a non-profit organisation bringing together innovation, science and civic engagement to protect the environment and strengthen climate resilience in Burkina Faso and West Africa.

Who we are

An alliance focused on solutions

Faced with the climate emergency and ecosystem degradation, ACIEA has chosen action. Founded in 2024, the Alliance brings together committed young people, scientific and technical profiles and field actors.

Our conviction: innovation, data and collective engagement make it possible to design sustainable solutions suited to local realities.

Our work

Six fields of action, one standard of excellence

Our initiatives are built around complementary areas where innovation and community engagement reinforce each other.

  • Climate and resilience
  • Water and sanitation
  • Biodiversity conservation
  • Digital innovation and artificial intelligence
  • Scientific research
  • Environmental education and community engagement
  • Completed
    Biodiversity conservation

    Tree planting and National Tree Day 2025

    Tree-planting actions carried out by the Alliance’s members and young people, including participation in National Tree Day 2025.

  • Completed
    Environmental education and community engagement

    Eco-Citizen Holiday Camp 2025

    Seven days of training and action in Komsilga to equip young people: soilless farming, recycling used tyres, eco-citizenship and tree planting.

    Komsilga, Kadiogo
  • Ongoing
    Water and sanitation

    SAAGDO — environmental monitoring and prevention of water-related pollution

    A digital solution combining a mobile app and a management platform for environmental monitoring, participatory mapping and the anticipation of water- and sanitation-related pollution risks.

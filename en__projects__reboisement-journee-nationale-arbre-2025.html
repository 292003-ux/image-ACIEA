Tree planting and National Tree Day 2025

Tree-planting actions carried out by the Alliance’s members and young people, including participation in National Tree Day 2025.

Context and rationale

Tree planting is one of the practical ways ACIEA mobilises young people for nature and climate resilience.

The Alliance published on its official page a video about its participation in National Tree Day 2025, along with photos of members planting seedlings.

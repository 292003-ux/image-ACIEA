Focus areas

Climate, water, biodiversity, digital innovation, research and education: six complementary areas for sustainable solutions adapted to local realities.

  • 01

    Climate and resilience

    Helping territories and communities adapt to the effects of climate change by combining local knowledge, awareness-raising and innovative solutions.

    →
  • 02

    Water and sanitation

    Working towards sustainable water resource management and better sanitation through environmental monitoring and pollution prevention.

    →
  • 03

    Biodiversity conservation

    Protecting ecosystems and biodiversity through tree planting, awareness-raising and the sustainable use of natural resources.

    →
  • 04

    Digital innovation and artificial intelligence

    Harnessing digital tools, data and artificial intelligence for environmental action and decision support.

    →
  • 05

    Scientific research

    Grounding action in knowledge: data collection, analysis and cooperation with research actors.

    →
  • 06

    Environmental education and community engagement

    Training, raising awareness and mobilising young people and communities so that everyone becomes an actor of the ecological transition.

    →
ACIEAAlliance for Climate Innovation and Ecological Action

A non-profit organisation based in Ouagadougou, ACIEA mobilises innovation, science and youth engagement for the climate, water and ecosystems.

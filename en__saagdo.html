SAAGDO : putting environmental data to work for action.

SAAGDO combines a mobile application and a management platform to strengthen environmental monitoring, citizen mapping and the anticipation of water- and sanitation-related pollution risks.

The challenge

Water and sanitation challenges that call for data

Preventing water- and sanitation-related pollution requires reliable, up-to-date and shared information. Several obstacles currently hold back action.

  • Water resource pollution

    Discharges, waste dumping and sanitation failures threaten water quality.

  • Information collection

    Field observations are scattered and rarely consolidated.

  • Environmental monitoring

    Regular monitoring of sensitive sites is hard to organise.

  • Data access

    Useful data often remains hard to access for stakeholders.

  • Community participation

    Citizens lack simple tools to report and follow up on issues.

  • Decision support

    Decision-makers need synthesised information to anticipate.

The solution

One digital solution, two complementary components

SAAGDO relies on two complementary components and three key functions, as described in the partnership with GWP West Africa.

  • Mobile application

    To contribute from the field and report observations.

  • Management platform

    To centralise, process and use information.

  • Participatory mapping

    To locate observations and share a common view of the territory.

  • Environmental monitoring

    To track how situations evolve over time.

  • Risk analysis and prediction

    To anticipate water- and sanitation-related pollution risks.

Partnership

A cooperation framework with GWP West Africa

On 3 July 2026, ACIEA and the Global Water Partnership West Africa (GWP-WA) signed, at the GWP-WA Secretariat in Ouagadougou, a Memorandum of Understanding on sustainable water resource management, climate resilience and digital innovation. The deployment of SAAGDO is at the heart of this partnership.
